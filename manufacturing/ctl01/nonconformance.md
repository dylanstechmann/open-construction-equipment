# Nonconformance and deviation control

**Unreleased template.** Nonconformance management can stop a process, not retrospectively make unsafe work acceptable.

## Identify and contain

Authorized personnel document the affected unit/item, BOM/drawing/process revision, objective requirement, observed actual result/evidence, discovery point, quantity/scope and potential other lots/units. Stop affected work and apply physical/quarantine and digital status controls through competent staff. Do not conceal, overwrite, clean, adjust, recharge, pressure test, retighten, reweld, power up, or continue assembly to make evidence look acceptable.

If a possible immediate hazard exists, only authorized site personnel implement the site emergency/isolation plan. An agent cannot physically contain an item or determine it is safe to continue.

## Required NCR fields

`NCR ID; unit/item/serials/lots; source and discovery date; requirement; observed condition; linked raw evidence; containment and scope search; hazard IDs/SR IDs; proposed disposition with technical rationale; affected documentation; independent approvals; rework/repair instruction revision; reinspection/retest; root cause; corrective action; effectiveness review; final signatures.`

## Dispositions

- **Rework to previously released requirement:** only via approved instruction and normally qualified process; record restored evidence.
- **Repair to a modified condition:** engineering-designed and approved repair with updated safety/manufacturing/service analysis and repeat verification; never informal weld/grind/fill.
- **Use-as-is/concession:** exceptional signed responsible-engineering and quality acceptance within legal limits, explicit unit scope and residual-risk review. Schedule pressure or ease of substitution is not rationale.
- **Return/scrap:** controlled segregation and disposition; battery/ hazardous-waste handling follows qualified applicable requirements.

No agent can approve use-as-is, repair, scrap release or restart. Merging an NCR document is not closure. NCRs affecting safety, protective structures, load paths, HV, braking, load holding, attachment retention, cleanliness, calibration, or software safety limits require responsible engineering review and an explicit hold-point release.

## Closure and learning

Confirm attached physical reinspection/retest evidence, recovered original identity, updated genealogy, scope search and all signatures. Quality personnel evaluate causes and systemic actions without assigning blame to defect reporters. Update hazard register, process controls, supplier status, documentation and training after review. Trend only genuine records; no project performance statistics are currently available.
