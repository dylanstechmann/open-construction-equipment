# WI-040: HV battery installation and isolation controls

## Scope and mandatory prerequisites

Future work for qualified HV/battery technicians and mechanical handlers at an approved site. This document does not instruct pack construction or energization. Required accepted pack with identity/lots/transport/storage and supplier evidence; released HV schematic/installation drawing; lifting/support and enclosure instructions; emergency/fire and damaged-pack response; isolation/LOTO and voltage-verification procedure; exact disconnect/protection/grounding/interlock/precharge/discharge design; rated inspected PPE/tools and calibrated meters selected for the final circuit.

Battery work requires competent evaluation of shock/arc risk even with a 96 V nominal target. Do not open modules or defeat BMS/HVIL/interlocks. Keep the pack isolated; a BMS indication or contactor state is not verified zero energy.

## Controlled sequence

1. Hold configuration and permit review: pack/drive/charger compatibility, all physical incoming and mount prerequisites, personal competence, emergency arrangements, site boundaries and uninterrupted LOTO ownership.
2. Inspect exterior damage/thermal or leakage indicators using the approved remote/non-energized method where provided. Stop and escalate damage; never troubleshoot by energizing.
3. Mechanically install with rated fixtures and released lifting/support points. Record pack/enclosure serials, actual joint results, restraint, clearances, protective barriers and service-disconnect accessibility.
4. Install only identified protected HV components and marked connections against released schematics. Verify connector keying, terminal workmanship and protection without inventing conductor sizes, fuse ratings or terminal torque.
5. Apply lockable isolation and verify all energy states using the approved proving-before/after measurement procedure. Map capacitors, charger backfeed, LV supplies and other sources; wait/discharge only as the released design directs.
6. Complete de-energized bonding, insulation, polarity, HVIL/contactor state and physical protection checks using approved methods that protect electronics and staff.
7. Remove tools/temporary covers only through permit control. Any later energization needs WI-080 and an independent G4 permit; this WI ends de-energized with hazardous covers controlled.
