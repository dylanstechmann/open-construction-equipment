# WI-010: Frame and lift-arm fabrication/welding

## Scope and mandatory prerequisites

For the exact released chassis/arm drawing and unit traveler **only** after G3 authorization. No OpenSCAD geometry or conceptual BOM is a drawing. Required shop risk assessment, approved material and cutting drawings, traceable heats, weld map, applicable WPS/PQR and consumables, qualified welder(s) whose qualification covers the actual process/material/position, ventilation/fire controls, calibrated dimensional/weld inspection tools, competent weld/NDT staff, and approved handling/support fixtures.

Before fit-up, quality verifies configuration, material identity/condition, WPS/PQR scope and welder qualification at a hold point. Released engineering defines dimensions/tolerances, joint preparation, weld sizes/lengths, preheat/interpass controls if any, heat input/current/polarity, sequence, restraint, distortion controls and acceptance criteria. None can be selected from this template.

## Controlled sequence

1. Issue traceable material and consumables; preserve heat/lot identity through cutting and kitting. Quarantine uncertain identity.
2. Inspect cutting/preparation, cleanliness, datum/fixture condition and joint fit-up against released drawings. Record actual geometry and keep the structure safely supported.
3. Obtain fit-up witness/hold acceptance before welds conceal or lock joints. Confirm weld-map IDs and inspection accessibility.
4. Weld only under the assigned WPS and approved sequence. Record welder, weld IDs, consumable lot and actual required process evidence. Stop for equipment, environment or material excursions.
5. Perform specified visual and non-destructive examination before coating/concealment. Mark results against the weld map without damaging identity.
6. Route nonconforming welds to engineering/quality. Grinding, gouging, repair welding or straightening requires an authorized repair instruction, renewed traceability and reinspection; do not use cosmetic finish to conceal defects.
7. Complete dimensional verification and preserve surface for the next coating gate only after structural acceptance.

## Hold/release and records

**H1:** materials/WPS/PQR/qualification/fit-up accepted. **H2:** every weld and NDT/repair accepted. **H3:** dimensional release to coating. Retain the signed traveler, heat and consumable genealogy, actual dimensions, weld/WPS/welder map, NDT raw evidence, NCR/repairs and independent responsible sign-off. No weld is represented as completed in this phase.
