# WI-030: Mechanical, undercarriage and attachment assembly

## Scope and mandatory prerequisites

For accepted coated structure, tracks/final drives, pivots, lift arms, seat/restraint/guards, quick attach and service supports. Required released assembly drawings, installation supplier manuals, lifting/support plans, fastener/joint schedule including grades/coatings/reuse/lubrication and derived tightening values (currently all TBD), pivot/wear criteria, calibrated torque/angular tools, rated lifting equipment, trained mechanical technicians and quality witnesses.

Verify center of gravity, fixture rating and support before handling. Never work beneath unsupported arms or suspended modules. Track tension systems and brakes may store energy; follow component-specific controlled service/assembly methods only.

## Controlled sequence

1. Match accepted component identities, revisions and supplier evidence to the traveler. Inspect mounting surfaces, preservation, bores/pins/bushings, seals, tracks and attachment retention features.
2. Assemble undercarriage/final drives without track release to motion. Record alignment, retention and actual released measurements; validate brake installation as a separate safety-critical check.
3. Install lift arms/pivots using engineered supports and controlled lifting. Record pin/retainer/bushing identity and actual fit/lubrication/retention inspections; no generic torque or clearance substitution.
4. Close safety-critical fasteners according to the released joint schedule. Capture every actual tightening result/curve as applicable, tool/calibration ID, conditioning/lubrication, operator and witness. Locking paint/markers supplement rather than replace torque evidence.
5. Install quick attach/seat/restraint/guards/steps using their structural and protective-system requirements. Check mechanical engagement through the intended range only after the appropriate staged verification permit.
6. Complete static geometric/engagement/retention/access inspections and foreign-object/tool removal before later energized checks.

## Gates and retained records

**H1:** installation identity/support/fit prerequisites accepted. **H2:** safety-critical joint/pin/brake/retainer/attachment records accepted before concealing or energized stages. **H3:** static return to subsequent process after quality/qualified engineering review. Any play, interference, damaged coating, substituted fastener or unapproved lubricant opens an NCR. Keep all actual records in unit genealogy.
