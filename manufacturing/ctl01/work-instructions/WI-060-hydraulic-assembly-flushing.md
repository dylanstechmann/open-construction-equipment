# WI-060: Hydraulic assembly, flushing and pressure-test controls

## Scope and mandatory prerequisites

Future work for qualified hydraulic technicians under released circuit/hose drawings, supplier cleanliness requirements, fluid/material compatibility, lift/load-control design, isolated assembly/flush/pressure-test procedures and approved spill/emergency plans. Required clean assembly area, genuine capped clean components, matched hose/crimp equipment and traceable assemblies, compatible filtered new fluid, controlled flush rig/filter/sampling equipment and calibrated particle/pressure instruments.

Working/test/relief pressure, flow, flush velocity/time/temperature, cleanliness code/sample limits, port size/thread, crimp and fitting torque are derived release values; none is supplied here. Do not start a pump to flush or test against this template. Pressure test requires guarded design, exclusion zone, overpressure protection, leak-search by approved safe means and explicit test permit.

## Controlled sequence

1. Review components/ports/ratings/certificates and clean assembly location. Record unique hose/cylinder/valve identities and keep protective caps until immediate controlled connection.
2. Assemble using released circuit/hose schedules and manufacturer requirements, with wear/abrasion/heat/bend/movement clearance and positively retained fittings. Log every open port and reclosure; prohibit unapproved adapters.
3. Inspect lift load-control devices, hose burst routing, test/service ports, isolation points, mechanical arm support and drain paths before fluid admission. Verify zero energy and stored-energy isolation map.
4. Configure flush loop per approved procedure, protecting or isolating sensitive components only as engineering specifies. Record temporary configurations and require formal restoration; never leave bypasses or jumpers for operation.
5. Use identified filtered fluid and flush media under actual measured conditions until the released **measured cleanliness** acceptance is met; elapsed time alone cannot pass. Retain samples/results and chain-of-custody.
6. After cleanliness sign-off and authorized restoration, conduct any pressure/leak/load-holding tests under a separately approved guarded sequence. Record actual calibrated curves/values and safe leak observations. Stop for pressure anomaly, whip, distortion, leakage or unexpected movement.
7. Drain/preserve/label as released, clean exterior spill residue, close FOD inspection and update fluid/hose/filter genealogy.

## Gates and retained records

**H1:** component/routing/clean assembly inspection. **H2:** measured cleanliness acceptance and temporary-loop restoration. **H3:** separate pressure-test permit and signed actual acceptance. No hand leak-search or tightening under pressure is allowed. After service, repeat applicable cleanliness and restoration gates rather than assuming original flush remains valid.
