# WI-020: Corrosion protection and coating

## Scope and mandatory prerequisites

Applies only to a structurally accepted component after the welding/dimensional gate. A released coating specification must identify substrate preparation, masking of bonds/datums/sealing surfaces, compatible coating system and edges/welds treatment, environmental and recoat/cure requirements, wet/dry film and continuity acceptance, handling time, color/safety labeling, repair procedure and disposal exposure controls. Coating technicians need relevant competence, respiratory/fire/environmental shop controls and calibrated environmental/profile/film gauges.

Coating can hide defects and can change earthing/bonding, pin fits, friction joints, QR attach mating and torque/preload behavior. No paint may be applied mechanically by assumption to these areas. Weld acceptance and subsequent structural verification are prerequisite hold points.

## Controlled sequence

1. Review structure release and NCR closure. Photograph/inspect required concealed details and preserve component/heat identity without destructive marking.
2. Inspect surface condition, contamination, drainage and access. Complete approved masking for electrical bonds, sealing, connection and controlled-fit areas.
3. Perform released preparation with environmental controls; record actual cleanliness/profile/dust or soluble-contamination results only where specified. Hold if dew-point/environmental requirements cannot be established.
4. Mix/apply by issued coating lot and released supplier/process requirements. Record technician, batch, times and actual environmental/process conditions; do not infer cure from elapsed generic shop time.
5. Measure released coverage/thickness/continuity and inspect runs, voids, holidays, adhesion evidence where specified, edges and masking removal. Keep unit supported during cure.
6. Restore required bond/contact surfaces only per released drawing; protect exposed bare steel by an approved method that preserves function. Repair defects under controlled repair instruction and reinspect.
7. Apply identification/safety labels only from the released label schedule and after underlying product acceptance.

## Gates and retained records

**H1:** substrate/preparation accepted before coating. **H2:** final film, masking cleanup, bond/contact and repair acceptance before assembly. Retain released specification, actual environmental/surface/film results, batch lots, instruments/calibration, photos/NDT cross-references, repairs and signed reviewer. Appearance alone is not evidence of coating performance.
