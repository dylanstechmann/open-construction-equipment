## Requirement change control

Any change to use, occupancy, contents, location, loads, fire strategy, water exposure, access, or autonomous operation requires a new applicability and hazard review. A future safe-room/storm-shelter or habitable proposal is a different project requiring new requirements and specialist design; it cannot be created by relabeling BUNKER-01.
