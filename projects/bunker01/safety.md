# BUNKER-01 preliminary safety and hazard boundaries

> **NO ENTRY OR EXCAVATION AUTHORIZATION.** This generic document is not a risk assessment, permit, engineered protective system, confined-space program, rescue plan, lift plan, construction procedure, or approval to operate equipment. Conditions and laws at a real site govern. Stop and obtain competent help rather than inferring instructions from this baseline.

## Required responsible roles

Before relevant work, the owner must engage appropriately qualified and, where required, licensed professionals: geotechnical, structural, civil/drainage, electrical, mechanical/ventilation, fire/life-safety, environmental and lightning specialists; surveyors; official utility locators; a competent excavation person; confined-space/atmospheric-safety and rescue personnel; lifting, formwork/falsework, reinforcement and concrete specialists; and autonomous-system safety engineers. The AHJ and other legally responsible authorities retain their own acceptance roles.

These roles must coordinate rather than approve isolated drawings. Permits, official utility locates, property authorization, released design, engineered excavation protection, inspections, atmospheric testing/ventilation and rescue plans where applicable are prerequisites, not optional paperwork. No agent can act as a competent person, witness, engineer of record, entry supervisor, qualified electrician, lift director, or AHJ.

## Hazard and control agenda

The table identifies topics for a site-specific risk assessment; it is not an exhaustive safe-work method. Controls require detailed procedures, competence, equipment and verification before physical work.

| Hazard / exposure | Required assessment and control boundary |
|---|---|
| Excavation collapse, sliding material and engulfment | Competent-person soil/condition assessment; engineered sloping, benching, shoring, shielding or other protection; control surcharge, vibration, adjacent structures and water; inspected access and changing-condition reviews. CAD working space is not a safe slope or clearance. Keep people and spoil/equipment out of unassisted collapse zones. |
| Buried or overhead utilities | Official locates and utility-owner coordination, records review, approved positive identification and isolation/support where required, controlled work around services and emergency contacts. Never rely on a historical drawing or simulation as proof of absence. |
| Confined space, oxygen deficiency/enrichment and toxic/flammable gases | Assess each construction and service space under applicable rules; control unauthorized entry; use calibrated atmospheric testing, clean-source ventilation, attendants/permits and rescue arrangements where required. Sensors and ventilation do not create a blanket entry clearance. Do not attempt unplanned rescue. |
| Water ingress, flooding and drowning/engulfment | Evaluate groundwater, precipitation, surface runoff, discharge failure and power loss; provide engineered water controls, monitoring and emergency egress. Never enter a flooding or potentially flooding space to recover property. Dewatering/discharge needs environmental and ground-stability review. |
| Falls and restricted access/egress | Engineer edges, openings, covers, access systems, landings and guardrails; maintain required escape routes and account for poor visibility, smoke, water and equipment blockage. A door or ladder drawn in concept is not evidence of safe egress. |
| Lifting and suspended loads | Qualified lift planning, ground/outtrigger assessment, rated equipment/rigging, inspection, competent operators/signalers, utility clearance and exclusion zones. No lift points, capacities or rigging selections are supplied. |
| Formwork, falsework and reinforcement | Engineer formwork/falsework and construction loads; control impalement/cuts, instability, manual handling and premature striking. Inspect released reinforcement, embeds, cover and supports before placement; concept CAD contains no reinforcement design. |
| Concrete placement and curing | Specialist placement plan for pump/truck interfaces, hose movement, pressure/release, washout, skin/chemical exposure, placement sequence, consolidation, temperature, testing and cure. Protect formwork and fresh concrete from unauthorized loads. No mix or strength is specified here. |
| Respirable crystalline silica and other dust | Assess cutting, drilling, excavation and cleanup exposures; implement applicable exposure-control plan, competent oversight, engineering/work-practice controls, restricted areas, hygiene and respiratory-protection program where required. |
| Noise and vibration | Assess equipment, compaction and concrete work exposures and effects on workers, neighbors, structures and buried services; select controls, monitoring and communication suitable for actual conditions. |
| Electrical energy and temporary power | Qualified design/installation, protected supply, grounding/bonding, suitable enclosures/cables, isolation and lockout/tagout, inspection and test. Treat construction power, permanent power, controls and stored energy separately; keep water and unauthorized persons controlled. |
| Battery and charging fire | Assess specific battery chemistry/system, charger, BMS, thermal propagation, ventilation, spacing, detection, isolation, responder guidance and damaged-battery handling. Charging location is unresolved; do not charge fleet batteries in the enclosure from this concept. |
| Lightning and external electrical hazards | Perform site risk assessment and coordinate any lightning protection with grounding, utilities, metalwork, electronics and construction activities. Do not treat reinforcement or a ground rod concept as a complete protective system. |
| Entrapment and security-versus-escape | Assess inward/outward opening, hardware, power loss, smoke, debris, unauthorized locking and rescuer access. Security must not defeat required inside escape or emergency release. Provide controlled external security without publishing sensitive details. |
| Autonomous fleet interaction | Separate autonomy from independent protective functions; validate detection and safe states for people, edges, support, utilities, water, poor visibility and changing ground. Use physical segregation, supervised permits and recovery plans; no current field autonomy is authorized. |
| Bystanders and public interface | Establish controlled site boundaries, delivery routing, signage, competent supervision and exclusion from excavation, lifting, charging and machine zones. Electric drive does not remove crush, run-over, stored-energy or unexpected-motion risks. |
| Environmental/archaeological/contamination discovery | Define stop-work, isolation, notification and qualified-disposition responsibilities for unknown drums, contamination, gases, protected habitat, artifacts or unexpected utilities. Do not investigate suspect materials by unqualified entry or handling. |
| Structural distress and premature loading | Establish inspection and acceptance for cracks, leakage, movement, formwork removal, backfill, equipment installation and changing loads. Monitoring thresholds and repair methods require responsible engineers. |

## Human gates and evidence

All [BG gates](construction/quality-gates.csv) are unresolved. Their acceptance requires actual documents, field conditions, raw evidence, named reviewers and dated signatures as applicable. Documentation generated by an AI agent cannot satisfy them. Passing repository checks is not a physical inspection and never changes `constructionAuthorized:false`.

Before ground disturbance, at minimum the requirements/use, site/legal, utility-locate, geotechnical, environmental and applicable structural/protective-system gates must be explicitly accepted in the correct sequence. Before any confined-space entry, the responsible employer/entry organization must separately classify and authorize that exact entry under applicable requirements. Gate acceptance is limited to its stated configuration and does not authorize later stages automatically.

## Emergency and flood thinking

A future site plan must identify emergency contacts, reliable communications, responder access, location information held outside the public repo, isolation points, accounting for personnel, and trained rescue arrangements. Flooding, fire, battery events, collapse, utility strike, entrapment, power/ventilation failure and loss of communications require scenario-specific planning and drills where appropriate.

The safe response to deterioration may be exclusion and professional assessment rather than entry. No one should enter a suspect atmosphere, flooded enclosure, unstable excavation or fire scene to retrieve equipment. Emergency response instructions must come from responsible local specialists and authorities for the actual systems; this concept deliberately supplies no universal hazardous-work procedure.

## Mothballing and safe abandonment

Mothballing or abandonment needs an approved plan before conditions deteriorate. Qualified parties must assess and control electrical/battery and other stored energy, remove or secure hazardous contents, prevent water accumulation and unauthorized entry, preserve required ventilation/monitoring or safely decommission it, maintain structural and excavation stability, manage environmental liabilities, and update emergency responders and records.

Permanent closure or demolition may require new permits, utility disconnection, structural/geotechnical review, waste characterization, controlled fill/demolition design and final site restoration evidence. Simply locking, burying, flooding, draining, sealing vents, or walking away is not an acceptable abandonment strategy. Reopening requires a fresh structural, atmospheric, electrical, fire, egress and legal assessment; a previous handover does not authorize re-entry.
