# BUNKER-01 reference starting points

These are official or standards-publisher starting points for future qualified review, **not a compliance matrix and not construction guidance for this concept**. URLs were checked as identifiable official pages on **2026-11-15** unless noted. No current standard edition, adopted amendment, clause or proprietary text was reviewed or is reproduced. The actual jurisdiction may use different rules and editions.

## Worker safety and construction — U.S. starting points

- OSHA, **29 CFR 1926 Subpart P — Excavations**: <https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926SubpartP>. Covers excavation-specific requirements and protective-system resources; a competent person must assess the actual work. State Plans and other jurisdictions may differ.
- OSHA, **29 CFR 1926 Subpart CC — Cranes and Derricks in Construction**: <https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926SubpartCC>. Starting point for lifting equipment, ground conditions, power-line and work-area controls.
- OSHA, **29 CFR 1926 Subpart K — Electrical**: <https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926SubpartK>. Construction electrical starting point, including batteries/charging and circuit lockout/tagging topics.
- OSHA, **29 CFR 1926 Subpart AA — Confined Spaces in Construction**: <https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926SubpartAA>. Apply only after competent scope/classification assessment during construction.
- OSHA, **29 CFR 1910.146 — Permit-required confined spaces**: <https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.146>. Potential general-industry/service-phase reference after assessment; its own scope excludes construction employment, so do not substitute it for Subpart AA.
- OSHA, **Respirable crystalline silica — construction**: <https://www.osha.gov/silica-crystalline/construction>. Official topic page and links to applicable construction exposure-control material.
- OSHA, **Control of hazardous energy**: <https://www.osha.gov/control-hazardous-energy>. Starting point for energy-isolation program assessment; construction-specific and local requirements also need review.

## Concrete, electrical and fire — publisher starting points

- American Concrete Institute, **ACI Store**: <https://www.concrete.org/store.aspx>. Qualified designers should locate the applicable adopted/current editions and errata for **ACI 318** (structural concrete), **ACI 301** (concrete construction specifications), and **ACI 347** (formwork), where applicable. The store listing is only a catalogue reference; no ACI edition has been purchased or reviewed for BUNKER-01.
- National Fire Protection Association, **NFPA 70 / National Electrical Code development page**: <https://www.nfpa.org/70>. Determine the locally adopted electrical code and amendments with the AHJ and qualified electrical designer. NFPA 70 is not self-executing evidence of installation safety.
- International Code Council, **Codes and Standards**: <https://www.iccsafe.org/products-and-services/codes-standards/>. Starting point for identifying locally adopted building, fire, mechanical, plumbing and related codes; adoption and amendments are jurisdiction-specific.

## Future safe-room/storm-shelter evaluation only

BUNKER-01 is **not** a safe room or storm shelter. The following links are reserved solely for evaluating a possible future, separately scoped occupant-protection project with qualified designers and the AHJ. Their presence does not claim current compliance, suitability for occupancy, flood safety, or protection from any event.

- FEMA, **Safe Rooms**: <https://www.fema.gov/emergency-managers/risk-management/building-science/safe-rooms>. Official portal for FEMA safe-room guidance; use it to locate the applicable editions of **FEMA P-320** and **FEMA P-361** if a future residential/community safe-room evaluation is authorized. The official page was identified through web search on 2026-11-15; direct automated retrieval was not used as evidence of review.
- FEMA, **Safe Room Publications and Resources**: <https://www.fema.gov/emergency-managers/risk-management/building-science/safe-rooms/resources>. Official publication index for FEMA P-320/P-361. No prescriptive residential design from these publications may be copied into this non-habitable utility concept.
- International Code Council, **IS-STM Consensus Committee on Storm Shelters**: <https://www.iccsafe.org/products-and-services/standards-development/is-stm/>. Official committee/publication route for **ICC/NSSA 500**. Determine the applicable adopted edition only during a separate storm-shelter design process.

## Required source-control practice

Responsible designers must obtain authoritative codes, standards, local amendments, manufacturer instructions and geotechnical/site records; record exact edition/revision and applicability; and preserve copyrighted material outside the public tree with access metadata. A citation, catalogue entry, free preview, purchased standard, or agent summary is not a signed engineering review or conformity claim.
