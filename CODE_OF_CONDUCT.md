# Code of Conduct

Open Construction Equipment is a learning community built around candor, especially about risk and uncertainty.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive across experience levels and identities.
- Critique designs and evidence rather than people; welcome correction and independent review.
- State assumptions, limitations, conflicts of interest, sources, and AI assistance honestly.
- Treat unsafe suggestions, fabricated evidence, and pressure to skip review as serious problems.
- Respect privacy, licenses, authorship, and the decision of maintainers to pause work for safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized or demeaning conduct, sustained disruption, retaliation for a good-faith safety concern, knowingly falsified engineering/test evidence, and encouraging dangerous physical actions outside approved procedures are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use the repository host’s private maintainer contact or security-reporting route when available. Do not include exploit details, personal information, or imminent physical hazards in a public issue. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, reject changes, suspend participation, or seek host/platform support proportionately. Immediate danger should be handled by the responsible local emergency/site organization, not this repository.

Maintainers will recuse themselves when their own conduct is disputed where a practical independent route exists. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project. It is an original project policy and is not a certification or legal process.
