# References and source notes

External resources are references for future qualified review, not evidence that OCE-CTL-01 meets them. Links were checked as identifiable official publisher/project locations on **2026-11-15** unless stated otherwise. Exact standards editions and clauses remain TBD; catalogue links are used deliberately to avoid implying an edition.

## Machinery and engineering sources

- International Organization for Standardization, **ISO Online Browsing Platform**, <https://www.iso.org/obp>. Search official catalogue entries for ISO 12100, ISO 17757, ISO 19014, ISO 20474, ISO 15817, ISO 13849-1, ISO 13850, ISO 4413, ISO 3471, ISO 3449, and ISO 2867. ISO owns the standards text; access may be paid or restricted.
- International Electrotechnical Commission, **IEC Webstore**, <https://webstore.iec.ch/>. Search for the official current IEC 60204-1 publication and national adoption.
- European Commission, **Machinery legislation / harmonised standards information**, <https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/mechanical-engineering/machinery_en>. Potentially useful only as one jurisdictional starting point; no European conformity is claimed.
- U.S. Occupational Safety and Health Administration, **Control of hazardous energy (lockout/tagout)**, <https://www.osha.gov/control-hazardous-energy>. Reference for later U.S. workplace analysis; other jurisdictions and actual site rules must be assessed.

## BUNKER-01 built-environment references

The civilian non-habitable utility-enclosure concept has a separate [official reference starting-point register](../projects/bunker01/references.md) for excavation, lifting, construction electrical, assessed confined-space, concrete, electrical/fire and future safe-room evaluation paths. Those pointers are not evidence of current-edition review, applicability, compliance, occupancy approval, or protective performance. BUNKER-01 is not a safe room or storm shelter.

## Supplier and standards evidence policy

Final component specifications must cite dated manufacturer datasheets/manuals by exact revision, including safety manuals where relevant. Any openly reused geometry, media, dataset, or software needs its source, author, version, and license recorded here. No supplier part number, attachment standard compatibility, engineering result, or certification is inferred from marketing material. No editorial reference to a standard licenses its reproduction.
