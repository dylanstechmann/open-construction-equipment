# PR-002: Safe shutdown, LOTO and stored-energy verification

## Purpose and prerequisites

Framework for unit-specific hazardous-energy isolation before service. The responsible qualified service lead must first issue an approved energy-control plan identifying **every** source and isolation point, lock ownership, transfer/shift rules, test methods, arc/shock/fluid/crush PPE, machine support, safe access, emergency route and staffing. Normal shutdown, software stop, key removal, e-stop actuation, or an open-contactor indication alone is not LOTO.

Only trained authorized people with actual electrical/hydraulic/mechanical competence perform their respective steps. Stop if the machine is unstable, damaged, burning, gassing or outside the plan and follow site emergency procedures rather than improvising isolation.

## Controlled planning sequence

1. Review exact serial/configuration, schematics/circuit, stored-energy map, work scope and previous energy state. Choose an approved lowered/stable attachment configuration, level approved support, parking/holding and any wheel/track restraint from the released manual before leaving the operator station.
2. Warn/coordinate with affected people, establish exclusion and prevent remote/site charging or control connections. Use the released normal stop where safe, then account for any suspended or pressurized assembly before leaving access.
3. Apply individually controlled lockout/tagout to all sources identified by the released plan: charger/external supply, traction/HV pack disconnect(s), LV supplies, drives/control, hydraulic pump/isolators and other mechanical/gravity/spring/thermal sources where required. Keep keys under authorized personal control and log transfer under policy.
4. Lower, restrain or mechanically support raised arms/attachments using released rated supports. Relief of one pressure gauge/volume is not proof of zero pressure in blocked cylinder/load-control circuits. Do not open lines to “check.”
5. Dissipate electrical/hydraulic/gravity/thermal/spring/track tension only using the released engineered procedure and designed points. Do not short capacitors or vent/loosen hydraulic components as a discharge strategy.
6. **Verify zero energy:** qualified personnel test every identified source at designated accessible points with rated suitable instruments/tools. Approved HV practice includes proving the voltage detector against a known source before and after the zero check as required; record actual readings and capacitor/backfeed conditions. Mechanically verify support/restraint and use approved pressure/state checks for trapped volumes.
7. Try/start/restart attempt only where the approved isolation plan explicitly prescribes it, from a safe position with controls returned to off/neutral afterward. It proves no start under those conditions, not absence of stored energy.
8. Record locks/tags, source IDs, supports, measurements, instrument/proving-device IDs and calibration, qualifications, time and verifier. Work stays within authorized scope.

## Interruption and restoration

Any new person, scope change, unexpected voltage/movement/pressure/heat or loss of lock control is a stop and reassessment. Tools left inside, temporary grounds/jumpers, test equipment and remote connections are inventory-controlled. Removal of locks, opening the exclusion and staged re-energization belong to PR-008 under each person’s site lock-removal authority; never simply reverse these generic steps.
