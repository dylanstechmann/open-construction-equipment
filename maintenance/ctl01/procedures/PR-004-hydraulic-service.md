# PR-004: Hydraulic system service

## Scope and competence

Qualified hydraulic/mechanical personnel only after PR-002, approved lower/support arrangement, released circuit and component/hose manuals, clean service area, compatible fluid/tools, spill/leak/injury response and measured stored-pressure procedure. Fluid injection can be catastrophic without a dramatic wound; any suspected injection requires immediate appropriate emergency medical response and fluid/product information.

Never loosen fittings or search leaks by hand under pressure, clamp a hose as routine isolation, or rely on the main relief setting/valve position/gauge zero to establish service safety. Raise/lower work requires released mechanical arm support independent of hydraulics.

## Controlled service flow

1. Identify circuit, line IDs and loads, including cap/rod ends, check/counterbalance valves, accumulator-like trapped volumes if any, return/case/cooling circuits and heating. Record pressure/temperature observations only through approved test points by competent staff.
2. Unload and support arms/bucket/attachment mechanically, isolate pump and all power/control sources, and release each designated trapped volume by the specific released method. Verify actual pressure/zero energy at designated points; no port opening to test.
3. Allow safe cool-down and contain fluid using environmental/site methods. Keep cabinets/panels stable and prevent contamination of open lines. Injuries or uncontrolled spray are emergency events, not cleanup-only tasks.
4. Replace/service only exact hose/cylinder/valve/filter/cooler items by released drawings and selection data. Record line/component/fluid/seal/filter lots, seal/cap identity, port condition and every open-point closure. Never substitute pressure rating, fitting thread/form or fluid because parts look similar.
5. Preserve cleanliness: clean tools/containers, cap immediately, avoid lint/prohibited cleaning supplies, protect from ingress and use only approved filtered new fluid. Never reuse drained fluid unless a released quality process permits it.
6. Restore routing/guards/bend/movement clearances/clamps, cylinder mounts, load-control valves, arm supports and reservoir/filter state as required. Record actual component-specific tightening results only from released joint data.
7. Determine refill/flushing/air management and load-holding verification from component/OEM engineering. Fluid loss, circuit opening or contamination triggers the released cleanliness/sample/retest scope, not a generic short flush.

## Return gate and evidence

Separate approved guarded pressure/leak/functional commissioning may be required. It has calibrated instruments, rated setup/exclusion, no hand leak checking and no standing under load. Retain actual pressure/cleanliness/drift where required, fluid/filter lots and unit genealogy. PR-008 must confirm all supports, test fittings and temporary items removed at the authorized time, hydraulics restored and residual leakage/function resolved before release.
