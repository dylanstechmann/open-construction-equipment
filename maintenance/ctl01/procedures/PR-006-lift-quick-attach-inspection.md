# PR-006: Lift-arm and quick-attach inspection/service

## Scope and competence

Initially external trained-operator inspection; detailed measurement/removal/repair is qualified mechanical/structural work under PR-002. Exact lift geometry/attachment ICD, released wear gauges/limits, rated arm/attachment supports, depressurized hydraulics and controlled removal plan are required. No bucket/attachment coupling dimensions, load limits or weld repair instructions are released.

Never work/reach under an unsupported arm/bucket or between an uncontrolled attachment and loader. Indicator `engaged` does not excuse inaccessible severe wear or damage; visual hooks alone do not prove each locking path.

## Inspection and service flow

1. Identify loader, attachment, coupler ICD/compatibility and loaded event history. Set down/unload using the approved method; isolate and mechanically support all gravitational movement before close inspection.
2. Clean safely enough to inspect without surface blasting that damages evidence or creates contamination. Record original cracks/deformation, unusual wear patterns, corrosion and repaired areas.
3. Inspect arm/chassis/bucket welds and parent material, pivot bores/pins/bushings/seals/retainers, cylinder eyes/mounts/rods, hoses/guards and side-to-side condition. Apply released dimensional/wear/alignment criteria; log actual values and locations.
4. Inspect coupler engagement faces, hooks/wedges/pins/locks/actuators/springs/sensors and manual release accessibility. Follow the released partial-engagement, unlocked, static retention and indication tests with attachment supported and area excluded.
5. Use qualified NDT where engineering requires for cracks/ROPS/arm/chassis components. Do not grind out cracks, drill holes, replace only one ill-fitting part or weld/repair under a generic procedure. Structural and protective-system issues create a hold for engineering repair instructions.
6. Replace pins/bushings/wear/retention/sensor parts only to exact released specification, with supported mechanical assembly, actual torque/fit/lubrication evidence and all retainers present. Retainer substitutes are prohibited.
7. Check other attachments only against a released interface/mass-CG/inertia/load center and retention envelope. “Universal-style” offers no compatibility approval. Any unknown attachment remains excluded.

## Return gate

Complete PR-008 including the required load-free retention/functional checks before any separately authorized loaded proof/stability activity. Retain inspection maps, actual wear/NDT data, photos, parts/joint genealogy and engineering/service sign-off. A sensor reading or paint mark does not substitute for mechanical retention and signed test evidence.
