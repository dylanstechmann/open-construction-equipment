# PR-001: Pre-start inspection

## Applicability and competence

Proposed before-each-use checklist for a **future released** manual machine, used by a trained authorized operator within approved site rules. It does not qualify an operator or cover every attachment/site. No pre-start or operation is currently authorized. Any proposed daily check is subordinate to the final OEM manual and law.

If machinery is in an unsafe position, moving, suspended, damaged, hot, smoking, gassing or leaking, do not approach for a closer check. Establish the site exclusion/emergency response and call responsible personnel.

## From a safe, authorized observation position

1. Match machine/attachment ID and the approved manual; check service due status and open defect/LOTO notices. Never remove another person’s lock/tag.
2. Assess authorized work area, ground/slopes, visibility, people/animals, overhead/underground utilities, weather, charging equipment and recovery/communication arrangements. Do not use proximity sensors as the sole bystander control.
3. Walk around only if safe: inspect chassis/arms/ROPS-FOPS zone for cracks/deformation/corrosion, attachment engagement/wear, guards/steps/seat/restraint, tracks/rollers, wheels/hubs where visible, leaks/residue, cable/hose abrasion, pack enclosure damage, loose items, labels and camera/sensor obstructions. Do not reach into pinch points or under an unsupported implement.
4. Check operator station for foreign objects, intact controls/indicators, neutral-demand condition and unobstructed exit; carry no unreviewed jumpers or bypass devices.
5. Check accessible cooling/fluids only by the released operator-level method. Caps, HV enclosures, pressure points and elevated components are not operator access items unless separately released.
6. Complete the approved functional sequence for brake/holding, manual controls, e-stop/interlocks, alarms, lights/horn and no-restart behavior, if the released manual includes operator tests. Unexpected actuation is a stop—not a retry opportunity.
7. Record findings in the unit log before leaving the site. Attachments or machine conditions outside their released ICD/manual are prohibited.

## Stop conditions and evidence

Any uncertain attachment lock, crack, brake anomaly, missing guard, interlock/e-stop failure, pack fault/damage, hydraulic leak/drift, track defect, invalid sensor/protective indication, overdue safety check or unexplained configuration change means **do not use**. Record actual observations and unit/configuration, reviewer/operator and required defect route; a checkbox `OK` cannot replace required measured functional results. A previous pre-start cannot be copied to a later shift.
